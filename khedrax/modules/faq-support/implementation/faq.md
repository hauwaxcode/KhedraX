# FAQ

## How do I create an account?
Create an account from the sign-up page using your work email address. After verification, you can sign in and start a trial.

## Can I invite teammates?
Yes. Workspace owners can invite teammates from the Team settings page and assign roles for access.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in screen to receive a reset email. If the message does not arrive, check spam or contact support.

## Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and export tools are available from the admin console.

## How do I change my billing plan?
Billing changes can be made from the Billing page in the workspace settings. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
